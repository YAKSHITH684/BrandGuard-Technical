# Acme Global Brand & Content Guidelines

## Section 1: Brand Voice and Tone
Acme communicates with authority, clarity, and genuine empathy.
- **Tone principles:** We are conversational yet precise. Avoid excessive corporate jargon, hyperbolic buzzwords, and vague promises.
- **Audience:** Enterprise engineering leads, CTOs, and compliance architects.
- **Reading Ease:** All public content must target a Flesch Reading Ease score of 60 or above. Avoid compound sentences with more than 35 words.

## Section 2: Prohibited Terms and Naming Rules
- The company name is **Acme**, never "ACME Corp", "Acme Inc.", or "acme".
- Refer to our enterprise tier as **Acme Enterprise Hub**, never "Acme Ultimate" or "MegaCloud".
- Deprecated product name: **HyperSync** was sunset in 2024. All materials must reference **Acme LiveSync**.
- Prohibited competitor disparagement: Never use the phrase "inferior legacy competitors" or "cheap alternatives".

## Section 3: Performance Claims Policy (Claims Policy 3.2)
- **Superlatives:** The words "fastest", "world's best", "#1", and "unbeatable" are strictly prohibited unless citing a designated third-party benchmark study with publication date.
- **Guarantees:** Never offer unconditional guarantees like "100% guaranteed" or "zero downtime guaranteed". Use "designed for high resilience" or "supported by our 99.9% uptime SLA".
- **Quantitative assertions:** Any percentage claim (e.g. "35% faster", "50% lower compute cost") must cite the approved benchmark study ID and fiscal year.

## Section 4: Privacy & Responsible AI Disclosures
- Any marketing copy describing AI capability must clarify that models are hosted in compliant sovereign environments and user data is never used for foundation model training without opt-in.
- Creators must never paste internal API keys, production tokens, or personally identifiable customer data into public drafts.

## Section 5: Accessibility and Design Standards
- All links must have meaningful descriptive text explaining their destination. Never use "click here" or "read more".
- Contrast ratio between text and background must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards: minimum 4.5:1 for standard body text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+ or 14pt bold).
- All image assets must include informative descriptive alternative text describing chart takeaways or diagram structure.
