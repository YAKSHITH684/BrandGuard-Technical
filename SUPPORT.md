---

## 3. What to Include When Requesting Support

To help us resolve your issue promptly, please provide:
1. **Operating Environment**: OS, Python version, Node.js version, and deployment platform (e.g. Render, Docker, local).
2. **Terminal Logs**: Relevant stack traces from Uvicorn or browser developer tools console logs.
3. **Reproduction Payload**: Sample draft copy or uploaded file causing the issue (ensure sensitive credentials are redacted).
4. **Current & Expected Outcome**: What happened versus what you expected to happen.
